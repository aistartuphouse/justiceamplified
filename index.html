The Origin of ALG

Built from necessity.

AO
Amber Ostergaard, Founder

ALG began when Amber Ostergaard faced complex litigation without the resources typically required to sustain expensive legal representation.

Rather than give up, she began developing AI-assisted workflows to help understand the case, organize information, research issues, and prepare litigation materials.

1,200+
hours of development and experimentation followed

She used the system to help organize her case, analyze issues, prepare litigation materials, research arguments, and assist with drafting court documents. The experience demonstrated something important: modern AI can dramatically increase the capabilities of people who otherwise may not be able to afford extensive traditional legal representation.

What if these capabilities could help millions of other people?
